# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

(Also packaged as an Electron desktop app for Windows and macOS. Its design language is the web app's own, not native.)

## Users

University students, starting with the maker and their classmates, studying for their own courses: lectures, readings and slides through a term, building up to midterms and finals. They study in long sessions at a laptop, often with the course PDF or Claude open beside their notes.

## Product Purpose

Appa keeps everything for a course in one place and turns it into what you need to know by the exam: decks of flashcards, notes written beside the slides, and the PDFs themselves. Success is a student who knows a deck by its due date without having had to plan how.

## Positioning

Organized by class. Anki and Quizlet are built around decks; Appa is built around the courses a student takes. Each class has its own homepage with its decks, notes and files together, and the class is how everything is found, studied and planned.

## Operating Context

- A term's worth of classes in the sidebar, each opened to its homepage of decks and notes.
- Reviewing cards daily (FSRS spaced repetition), with due-date plans that spread a deck's new cards until the exam.
- Writing Grid notes beside lecture PDFs or a browser (Claude), and polishing them with AI.
- Bringing cards in from Anki (.apkg) and Quizlet (pasted text).
- Used in the browser and as the desktop app, which updates itself.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Classes are top-level decks (name, optional emoji, user-arranged order); decks live inside them.
- Scheduling is FSRS with Anki-compatible imports and review history; each deck has its own settings, with presets kept out of sight.
- AI features (Polish, Fill the rest, Quiz, Learn) use the user's own OpenRouter key.
- Saved data must survive every update; old saved formats are read and converted, never dropped.
- Themes (light, dark, blue, purple, pink, custom colour) and the app font are per computer.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Appa. The app icon is `public/icon.png`.
- Voice: friendly and warm, like a study buddy: encouraging and a little playful, never noisy during study.

## Evidence on Hand

- The working app and its own sample content (the Grid notes example). No testimonials, user counts or reviews exist; don't invent any.

## Product Principles

1. The class is the home for everything: a student should never wonder where something for a course lives.
2. Studying comes first: nothing should distract during review or note-taking.
3. Plan for the student: due dates, daily limits and spreading new cards happen for them.
4. Friendly, not childish: warmth in the words and small touches, calm in the layout.
5. Never lose a student's work.
